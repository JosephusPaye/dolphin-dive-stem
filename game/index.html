How to Play

The objective of Dolphin Dive is simple: escape the approaching oil spill by swimming away for as long as you can.

Avoid hitting the junks as they slow you down. Collect starfish and you can use them for speed boosts.

  • < Main Menu
  • Next >

Directional arrows control your movement:

    Up Arrow: Swim upward

    Down Arrow: Swim downward

    Right Arrow: Speed Boost

  • < Back
  • Next >

On a touchscreen device, controls are even simpler:

    Tap top-left half of screen: Swim upward

    Tap bottom-left half of screen: Swim downward

    Tap right half of screen: Speed Boost
