How to Play

The objective of Dolphin Dive is simple: escape the approaching oil spill by swimming away for as long as you can.

Avoid hitting the junk as they slow you down. Collect starfish and you can use them for speed boosts.

  • < Main Menu
  • Next >

Directional arrows control your movement:

    Up Arrow: Swim upward

    Down Arrow: Swim downward

    Right Arrow: Speed Boost

  • < Back
  • Next >

On a touchscreen device, controls are even simpler:

    Tap top-left half of screen: Swim upward

    Tap bottom-left half of screen: Swim downward

    Tap right half of screen: Speed Boost

Dolphin Dive is a game raising awareness about the negative effects of pollution on dolphins and other marine life in the oceans.

Dolphin Dive was made by Anomaly Blue, a team of four from Callaghan College Jesmond Senior Campus:

  • Brian Burns: Developer and Audio Tech
  • Ben Mildren: Developer and Documentation
  • Josephus Paye II: Lead Developer
  • Heath Tyler: Artist and Audio Tech
